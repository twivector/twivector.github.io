Twivector Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 31, 2026

Twivector ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use our games, applications, websites, and related services. It applies to Twivector's games, applications, websites, and related services, including Basket Crush and Thorn Dash. Twivector may develop and operate additional games and services over time. Different products may use different features and third-party services, as explained below.

1. Basket Crush

The current version of Basket Crush does not include Google AdMob, Firebase Analytics, or other third-party advertising or analytics SDKs. It is not currently configured to send gameplay analytics or advertising data to those services.

Twivector does not currently operate a Basket Crush account system or require players to submit personal information to play the game. If these features or third-party SDKs are added in a future version, we will update this Privacy Policy to explain the relevant information practices.

Google Play and the device operating system may independently process information in connection with app distribution, installation, security, and platform services. Those practices are governed by the relevant platform providers' privacy policies and are separate from data collection by Twivector through Basket Crush.

2. Twivector Websites

Our websites may use third-party tools to understand website traffic, measure site performance, and support advertising. The website page containing this policy currently loads Google Analytics and Google AdSense scripts.

Depending on the website features and your settings, these providers may use cookies or similar technologies and may process information such as:

  • Browser and device information
  • Pages visited and interactions with website content
  • Approximate location inferred from network information
  • IP address and online identifiers
  • Advertising interactions and related measurement data

Google processes information collected through its services according to its own policies. More information is available in Google's Privacy Policy and Google's information about advertising technologies.

3. Twivector Games, Products, and Third-Party Services

Twivector develops and provides games and other digital products, including Basket Crush and Thorn Dash, as well as its websites and related services. This Privacy Policy applies to those products, subject to the product-specific practices described in this policy.

Other Twivector games or applications may use third-party services for functions such as analytics, advertising, diagnostics, or platform features. The services used can vary by product and version. Where such services are used, they may process information under their own privacy policies.

The presence of a service in this general policy does not mean that the service is integrated into Basket Crush or every Twivector product. We will update the relevant product disclosures when a service is added or its use materially changes.

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Twivector websites and their third-party providers may use cookies, pixels, tags, or similar technologies for website functionality, analytics, and advertising. You can manage or restrict cookies through your browser settings. Some website features may not work as intended if cookies are disabled.

5. Consent and Regional Privacy Rights

Where applicable law requires consent for certain website analytics or advertising technologies, we will use appropriate consent mechanisms. Advertising and analytics preferences may also be managed through available browser, device, or provider controls.

Your privacy rights depend on your location and applicable law. These rights may include requesting access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, or objecting to or restricting certain processing.

6. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly ask children to provide personal information directly to Twivector. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us using the details below. We will review the request and take appropriate steps where required by applicable law.

The intended age group and any child-directed features of an individual game or application are described through that product's applicable store and platform disclosures.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information that we process. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Data Retention and Privacy Requests

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, to meet applicable legal obligations, or to resolve disputes. Third-party providers may retain information in accordance with their own policies and retention practices.

To ask a question or make a privacy request concerning information handled by Twivector, contact us at twivector@gmail.com. Please describe the product involved and the nature of your request. We may need to verify your request before acting on it.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as our products, services, or legal obligations change. The revised version will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
